About David Bay

I'm a forensic accountant and business valuer, though I think of myself first as a business person.

Most of my career has been spent inside businesses rather than looking at them from the outside. I have owned and operated two franchise businesses, prepared more than 150 business plans for all sorts of businesses, and worked in analytical and CFO roles. Some of that was with large mining companies, which is about as big as business gets, though most of my work now is with small businesses, where the owner often is the business and the books were kept for the tax return rather than for anyone else.

That experience matters when you're deciding whether a business needs valuing. Reading small business accounts well takes someone who knows how those businesses actually run: where private costs tend to end up, why profit jumps around from year to year, and what a loan account is really saying. I have also worked as an analyst on a money laundering investigation alongside the AFP and the CDPP, and spent time as Assistant Listing Manager at the ASX, so I'm used to records that don't tell the whole story at first glance.

Much of my valuation work is for people going through a separation, which is why I wrote The 3am Book, a plain-English guide to the money side of separation for the partner who was less across the finances. I've also written Suddenly Responsible: Starting Out, a money guide for young Australians. I'm a member of the Australian Association of Collaborative Professionals and trained as a financial neutral, so I'm comfortable working with both of you together.
