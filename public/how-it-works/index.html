How it works

Four steps, one form, one payment, and a written answer within three to five business days of my receiving your documents.

  1. Complete the online form. Tell me about the business and who is involved, and tick the boxes confirming you understand what the Report is and isn't. If you're ordering together, add the other party's details so the Report is addressed to you both.
  2. Upload your documents. Usually three years of financial statements and tax returns, plus anything about related companies, trusts and loan accounts; see What business records do I need. Send what you have.
  3. Pay $997 including GST. One payment, made by one person, at the end of the form. Work starts once payment and documents are both in, and the fee is non-refundable from that point.
  4. Receive your Report. One of three written answers: a valuation is warranted, not warranted, or can't be decided on the records, prepared and signed by me.

If the answer is "can't be decided", the Report names the documents most likely to help. Send them in one batch within 21 days and I'll update the Report at no charge; after that the engagement is closed, and any further work is charged at my hourly rate with the scope agreed first.
