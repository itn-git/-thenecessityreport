Do I need a business valuation for my property settlement?

Short answerOften, though not always. If a business is part of the property pool, its value has to be agreed or worked out somehow, but a formal valuation is only one way of doing that, and for some small businesses it costs more than it is likely to tell you.

The Necessity Report™ answers this question for your situation, for a fixed fee of $997 including GST. It is not a valuation and gives no dollar figure; it tells you whether paying for one makes sense.

Why the question matters

A formal business valuation for a family law matter will often cost several thousand dollars, and more if it is prepared for court. That is money well spent when the business is worth arguing about, though it can be money wasted when the business is really one person's job, a ute and a set of tools. The trouble is that most separating couples, and quite a few advisers, cannot easily tell which of those two situations they are in until someone has looked at the figures.

When a valuation is usually worth having

  • The business makes more than a fair wage for the person who runs it, year after year.
  • It has staff, contracts or repeat customers who would stay if the owner left.
  • Its structure involves a company, a family trust or related businesses, so the value sits in more than one place.
  • Money moves between the owner and the business through loan accounts, private expenses or unusual payments.
  • The two of you disagree about what it is worth, and the gap is large compared with what a valuation costs.

When it may not be

  • The business earns roughly what its owner would earn as an employee, and little more.
  • Its main assets are equipment and vehicles that can be valued more simply.
  • You both already agree on a figure, and you have seen enough of the records to be comfortable with it.

These are signs rather than rules, and a business can tick boxes on both lists. That is exactly the situation where an independent look at the records before anyone commits to a valuation tends to pay for itself.
